Mobile PWA Debugging:

For Android:

  1. Open Chrome browser (not the PWA)
  2. Go to chrome://inspect/#devices
  3. Connect your phone via USB
  4. Enable USB debugging on phone
  5. Find your PWA and click "Inspect"

For iOS:

  1. Use Safari on macOS
  2. Enable "Develop" menu in Safari preferences
  3. Connect iPhone via USB
  4. Select your device from Develop menu
