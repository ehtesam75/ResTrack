About ResTrack

Student Performance Tracking System

🚀 About This Project

ResTrack (Results Tracking System) is a comprehensive student performance monitoring and analytics platform designed to help teachers and students track academic progress effectively.

💡

This project is being developed by a chatbot. I've put in considerable effort fixing logic issues, resolving bugs, and contributing feature ideas to ensure everything works properly.

🎯 Purpose

ResTrack transforms traditional grade tracking into an engaging, gamified learning experience. By converting exam performance into points and rewards, students are motivated to excel academically.

⚙️ How It Works

  • • Teachers enter exam results
  • • System calculates scores & grades
  • • Earn points for performance
  • • Bonus points for achievements
  • • Analytics show trends & rankings
