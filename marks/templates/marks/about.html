About ResTrack

Student Result Tracking & Analysis System

{# Project description card #}

🚀 About This Project

ResTrack (Results Tracking System) is a comprehensive student performance monitoring and analytics platform designed to help teachers and students track academic progress effectively. Teachers can create and schedule timed exams, while students can attend those exams in both online and offline modes — making it a complete assessment and tracking solution.

{# Purpose section #}

🎯 Purpose

ResTrack transforms traditional grade tracking into an engaging, gamified learning experience. By converting exam performance into points and rewards, students are motivated to excel academically.

{# How it works section #}

⚙️ How It Works

  • •Teachers enter results & schedule timed exams
  • •Students attend exams online or offline
  • •System calculates scores, grades & points
  • •Earn points convertible to real money (BDT)
  • •Monthly competitions with bonus rewards
  • •Manage exam papers & track documents
  • •Deep analytics show trends & rankings

{# Key Features card #}

✨ Key Features

🎯 Exam Center

Teachers can schedule and manage timed exams. Students can attend exams in both online and offline modes with live countdown timers, answer sheet submissions, and bonus time support.

📄 Exam Documents

Upload and manage question papers and marked answer papers. Students can look up any exam by ID to instantly access their documents.

📊 In-Depth Result Analytics

Comprehensive performance analysis with dual-threshold grading, subject-wise breakdowns, interactive charts, leaderboards, head-to-head student comparisons, excellence tracking, gamified points system (1 Point = 1 BDT), and monthly competitions.
