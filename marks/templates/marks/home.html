Get Started in Minutes

Two guided paths designed to move teachers and students from signup to measurable progress with less friction.

Teacher Journey

4 Steps
  1. 1

    Set up teacher account Create your teacher workspace

    Create your profile and start your workspace. Sign up once and set your profile so all classes, exams, and reports stay under your account.

  2. 2

    Add students and subjects Create student accounts and class data

    Create student logins and organize class info. Add students, subjects, and exam types so records stay organized from day one.

  3. 3

    Run exams and publish marks Conduct exams and publish marks

    Run tests, record marks, and share results. Run live/scheduled exams or add marks manually, then publish results with one workflow.

  4. 4

    Track progress Review progress and guide improvement

    Use reports to spot gaps and improve outcomes. Use dashboards, comparison views, and trend insights to support each student better.

Student Journey

4 Steps
  1. 1

    Get login from teacher Receive account credentials from your teacher

    Use the username and password provided by your teacher. Your teacher creates your student account and shares your username and password.

  2. 2

    Log in to your dashboard Log in and view your subjects

    See your subjects and upcoming exams. Sign in from phone or desktop to access your assigned classes and upcoming exams.

  3. 3

    Take your exams Take exams and submit on time

    Complete timed exams and submit before the deadline. Join live exams with timer support, submit answers, and complete each test confidently.

  4. 4

    See results and progress Track results, rankings, and growth

    Check marks and monitor your improvement. Check marks, compare performance, and follow your improvement after every exam.
