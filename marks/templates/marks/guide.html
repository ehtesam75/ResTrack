🚀 About This Project

ResTrack (Results Tracking System) is a comprehensive student performance monitoring and analytics platform designed to help teachers and students track academic progress effectively.

🎯 Purpose

ResTrack transforms traditional grade tracking into an engaging, gamified learning experience. By converting exam performance into points and rewards, students are motivated to excel academically while teachers gain powerful insights into class performance trends.

⚙️ How It Works

  • •Teachers enter exam results for students
  • •System calculates scores, grades, and points
  • •Earn points for good performance
  • •Bonus points for monthly achievements
  • •Analytics show trends and rankings

💰 Points System

Lifetime points are calculated based on exam performance and achievement bonuses. Points have real monetary value!

💵 Points = Real Money!

1 Point = 1 BDT (Bangladeshi Taka)

Every point you earn has real monetary value. Academic achievements = financial rewards!

💵 Withdrawals:

  • •Request withdrawal if points > 0
  • •Amount (BDT) provided by teacher
  • •Spent: Points already used
  • •Remaining: Available balance

Example: 100 lifetime - 30 spent = 70 points (70 BDT) available!

Exam Points

Each exam contributes points based on achieved grade. Cumulative sum forms your lifetime total.

Example: 88% on CQ (Superb) = +20 pts, while 45% MCQ (Poor) = -10 pts.

Bonus Points

Earn additional bonus points through achievements like monthly wins.

Total Lifetime Points

Formula: Lifetime Points = Exam Points + Bonus Points

Determines leaderboard position and reflects overall academic performance.

🏆 Monthly Wins Bonus

Students achieving first rank in any completed month receive a performance bonus.

🎖️ 40 Points per Monthly Win

Each month with first rank (highest avg score) earns a 40-point bonus.

🎯 Eligibility (Both Required)

  • •40% Attendance: At least 40% of month's unique exams
  • •Min 3 Exams: At least 3 unique exams attended

⚠️ Both conditions required to receive bonus, even with highest avg.

Monthly Ranking Method

  • •Only completed months evaluated
  • •Avg score calculated for all exams
  • •Highest avg = winner (if eligible)
  • •Each win = +40 bonus points

Example: First rank in Jan (5/8 exams), Feb (4/10), Apr (6/7) = 3 × 40 = 120 bonus points

⚡ Automatic Calculation

Bonus updates automatically when exams are added, edited, deleted, or points recalculated.

⭐ Excellence Rate

Excellence Rate measures the proportion of exams with excellent performance.

CQ Threshold

≥ 80%

MCQ Threshold

≥ 85%

📊 Calculation Formula

Excellence Rate = (Excellent Exams ÷ Total Exams) × 100%

Example: 7 excellent / 10 total = 70% excellence rate

Significance: Measures consistency in high-level performance. 80%+ demonstrates sustained mastery.
